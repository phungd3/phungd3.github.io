About

I am an Aerospace Engineering undergraduate at the University of California, Irvine and an undergraduate researcher in the eXploration Logistics & Control (XLC) Group, advised by Prof. Yuri Shimane.

My research focuses on spacecraft trajectory design and control in cislunar space, with particular interests in high-fidelity dynamical modeling, trajectory optimization, station keeping, and scientific software.

Research

Current research

High-Fidelity Earth–Moon Vertical Orbits

High-fidelity baseline design and station-keeping analysis for Earth–Moon vertical orbits near the collinear libration points. The work combines ephemeris dynamics, lunar gravity harmonics, sequential convex programming, model predictive control, and dynamical-systems analysis.
