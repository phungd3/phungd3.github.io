About

I am an Aerospace Engineering undergraduate at the University of California, Irvine and an undergraduate researcher in the eXploration Logistics & Control (XLC) Group.

My work focuses on spacecraft motion and control in cislunar space, particularly high-fidelity dynamical modeling, trajectory optimization, station keeping, and computational astrodynamics.

Current research · 2026–present

High-Fidelity Earth–Moon Vertical Orbits

Baseline design and station-keeping analyses for Earth–Moon vertical orbits near the collinear libration points using ephemeris dynamics, lunar spherical harmonics, sequential convex programming, and model predictive control.

Software

Contributions to open-source astrodynamics software for high-fidelity trajectory modeling, propagation, and analysis.

Julia · Contributor

HighFidelityEphemerisModel.jl

A Julia package for high-fidelity spacecraft dynamics and ephemeris-based trajectory propagation. My contributions include Gauss variational equation propagation, a Julia-native ephemeris backend, SPICE kernel generation workflows, and validation against independent propagators.

Julia · Contributor

AstrodynamicsCore.jl

A Julia package providing foundational astrodynamics utilities and orbital-state conversions. My contributions include validation of ordinary-equinoctial element conversions across representative orbit regimes, domain boundaries, and generic-number types.
